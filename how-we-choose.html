How we choose products

A smaller catalog takes more thought.

Every Mino recommendation starts with a simple question: does this genuinely help someone build a first kitchen that works?

The review process

A product has to earn its place.

We’re not trying to cover every brand or every version. We’re looking for useful products that make the decision easier.

  1. It begins as research.

    We start with the kitchen problem, not with a product listing.

  2. We look at everyday usefulness.

    Is it practical, durable, easy to clean, and sensible in a smaller space?

  3. We ask whether a beginner needs it.

    A good product can still be the wrong recommendation for a first kitchen.

  4. We consider the bigger setup.

    It should work well on its own and make sense inside a complete bundle.

  5. Only useful choices move forward.

    If it duplicates another tool or solves a problem most people won’t have, we leave it out.

Curated over crowded

The catalog is small on purpose.

Fewer options mean we can explain why each one is here—and you can spend less time comparing products that do nearly the same thing.
