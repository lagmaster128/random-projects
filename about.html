About Mino Kitchens

A kitchen shouldn't be this hard to figure out.

When I first started learning to cook, I quickly realized that finding recipes was easy—but understanding what I actually needed in my kitchen wasn't.

Most of what I learned came from asking family and friends. There wasn't a simple place that explained the fundamentals: which tools were truly essential, which ones could wait, and how to build a kitchen without wasting money on things I'd rarely use.

As I got older and started thinking about building my own kitchen, I ran into a different problem.

Shopping online felt overwhelming.

Large marketplaces offered thousands of products, endless reviews, and dozens of nearly identical options. Every purchase meant opening another tab, comparing another product, and wondering whether I was buying something I'd actually use.

It felt like building a kitchen required as much research as the cooking itself.

That's why I created Mino Kitchens.

Not to sell the most products.

To make building a kitchen simpler.

Every product is chosen because it solves a real problem, earns its place, and helps create a kitchen that's practical, organized, and enjoyable to use. Instead of asking you to sort through hundreds of choices, Mino focuses on thoughtfully curated essentials and bundles that work well together.

Our guides follow the same philosophy. Rather than teaching hundreds of recipes, they help you understand what you actually need to cook with confidence, organize your space, and avoid unnecessary purchases.

Whether you're moving into your first apartment, setting up a small kitchen, or replacing clutter with better essentials, Mino exists to help you build a kitchen with confidence.

Our Mission

Build your first functional kitchen with confidence.

To help people build their first functional kitchen through thoughtfully curated essentials, practical guidance, and complete solutions that reduce clutter, simplify everyday cooking, and help every purchase earn its place.

Spend less. Cook more.
